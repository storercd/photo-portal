TFP (Trade for Prints)

What is TFP?

TFP (Trade for Print / Trade for Portfolio) is a collaborative arrangement where a photographer and a model/subject pool their time, talents, and creative ideas. No money changes hands. Instead, both parties receive high-resolution, professionally edited images to build and elevate their respective portfolios.

Open for Ideas & Creative Pitches

Right now, I am actively looking to expand my experience in new settings, test out creative lighting, and collaborate with passionate people. Whether you are an experienced model needing updated headshots, an athlete looking for dynamic action shots, or someone who has never been in front of a camera before, I am open to your pitch!

Types of sessions I'm excited to shoot:

  • Sports & Athletic Movement: Cheer, gymnastics, basketball, running, or functional fitness. This includes live games or controlled photo shoots, focusing on an individual or an entire team/game. All options are welcome.
  • Casual & Environmental Portraits: Natural light, urban outdoor settings, or golden hour portraits.
  • Creative & Experimental Concepts: Unique lighting setups, high-speed movement, or styled themes.

How It Works

  • The Idea: You bring a vision, or we brainstorm a theme, location, and wardrobe together.
  • The Shoot: A relaxed, supportive session focused on getting high-quality, authentic images.
  • The Deliverables: You get a curated gallery of high-resolution, retouched digital photos with full usage rights for your personal portfolio and social media.

Model Release Required

To ensure both parties can freely share and use the photos for portfolios, websites, and social media, a standard model release form will be signed prior to the shoot (a parent/guardian signature is required for models under 18).
